FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between hazardous and non-hazardous waste?

Hazardous waste has properties that endanger health or the environment (toxic, flammable, corrosive, etc.) and needs special permits, packaging and transport conditions. Non-hazardous waste does not.

How do I know if my waste is hazardous?

It is determined by the waste code (list in the Waste Management Regulation) and, where needed, laboratory analysis. Our team will help you with this.
